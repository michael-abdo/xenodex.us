THE HYPOTHESIS

Semantic patterns in language can reliably infer personality traits

Our core hypothesis is audacious: Semantic patterns in language can be used to reliably infer latent personality traits with ≥99% accuracy from a 1-hour data source.

If we follow the plan, all things being equal, we will have a tool to execute on this claim. Not all phenotypic data is created equal - some traits are best determined via text, others via images. Our goal is to see consistent progression in the direction of accuracy.

The definite smallest working unit of 99% accuracy - that's our north star.

PHASE 1: AI GROUND TRUTH

Get AI working to reproduce ground truth

Our first goal is simply to get AI to accurately type personalities. But we must acknowledge the circular validation problem we're solving.

The Circularity Problem:

X
What We're Validating
  • • Consistency of human typing criteria
  • • AI's ability to learn those criteria
  • • Reliability of the typing system
!
What We're NOT Validating
  • • Whether cognitive functions exist neurologically
  • • Whether patterns map to biology
  • • Whether types predict beyond the system

We're essentially perfecting sophisticated pattern matching - valuable, but not empirical proof.

PHASE 2: THE CRITICAL TEST

Genotype-Phenotype Correlation

The DNA sequencing work is crucial - it's the make-or-break validation. Either we'll find genetic markers that correlate with our AI's typing patterns (suggesting real biological basis), or we won't (suggesting useful constructs but not fundamental biological categories).

Key Genetic Markers to Identify:

Primary Criteria:

  • Meaningful to stakeholders - Show don't tell
  • Rare, impactful genes - High-value discoveries
  • Physiological genes - Observable correlations
  • Relatively easy to test - Practical implementation

Further Criteria:

  • Known pleiotropic effects - Affecting multiple systems
  • High minor allele frequency - Common enough to study
  • Measurable intermediate phenotypes - Hormone levels, brain imaging
  • Cross-species validation - Similar effects in model organisms

This external biological validation will determine if we're detecting real signal or sophisticated pattern matching.

THE PERFECT NATURAL EXPERIMENT

Trans Individuals on HRT

The critical test involves sex hormone pathway genes and transgender individuals on hormone replacement therapy. This creates a perfect natural experiment for separating organizational (prenatal) from activational (current) effects.

The Two-Hit Hypothesis:

First Hit (Prenatal)

Testosterone organizes brain structures during weeks 8-24 of gestation:

  • • Hypothalamus structure
  • • Corpus callosum connectivity
  • • Hippocampal organization
  • • Language center lateralization
Second Hit (Puberty)

Hormones activate what was organized:

  • • Like turning on a pre-wired system
  • • Can modulate intensity
  • • Cannot change fundamental patterns
  • • Architecture remains stable

What HRT Changes vs. What It Can't:

What HRT Changes (Activational)
  • • Muscle mass, fat distribution
  • • Energy levels, mood patterns
  • • Sexual response, skin texture
  • • Current emotional intensity
  • • Social confidence, assertiveness
What HRT Can't Change (Organizational)
  • • Fundamental cognitive architecture
  • • Deep linguistic patterns
  • • Core processing preferences
  • • Spatial vs verbal thinking ratios
  • • Information organization strategies

The killer observation: cognitive patterns should remain stable even as the entire hormonal milieu inverts.

THE FOOLPROOF DESIGN

Why This Destroys Alternative Explanations

This experimental design eliminates every confounding variable. We track individuals before, during, and after hormone transitions to isolate organizational from activational effects.

Experimental Protocol:

1
Pre-HRT Baseline

Record 4+ sessions over months. Extract stable cognitive architecture and linguistic patterns.

2
During HRT Monitoring

Continue sessions every 2 weeks as hormones change. Watch activational features shift.

3
Validation

Deep cognitive patterns remain stable despite complete hormonal inversion.

Alternative Explanations Destroyed:

Social Conditioning

Same person, same history, only hormones changed

Current Hormonal State

Watching hormones flip completely, architecture persists

Self-Concept

Cognitive architecture doesn't reorganize despite feeling "more themselves"

Measurement Artifact

Before/during/after data from same person eliminates this

This is the most scientifically rigorous way to test whether personality types reflect biological reality.
