THE ATTEMPTS

The Honest Failures

Science has been circling this truth for decades. A study finds a correlation. Makes headlines. Falls apart under scrutiny.

The pattern repeats. Again and again.

Not because the patterns aren't real. But because researchers kept measuring shadows instead of substance. They found what culture created, not what biology encoded.

The Comfortable Explanations:

  • "It's just culture" - Tell that to identical twins raised apart who fear the same specific things
  • "It's how they were raised" - Explain adopted children who mirror birth parents they never met
  • "It's confirmation bias" - Then why do strangers guess your personality from baby photos?
  • "It's self-fulfilling prophecy" - How do babies fulfill prophecies before they can even understand them?
  • "It's not scientific" - Neither was continental drift. Until it was.

Every comfortable explanation crumbles when you look close enough.

THE BREAKTHROUGH

When Scale Reveals Truth

One redhead acting fiery? Stereotype. A thousand redheads across five continents sharing specific cognitive patterns?

Science.

Scale transforms anecdotes into evidence. Eliminates cultural noise. Reveals the signal hiding beneath.

The Unflinching Method:

  • Blind to beauty - Coders never see faces. Only behavior. No bias can creep in.
  • Multiple observers - Different eyes, same patterns. Reliability you can measure.
  • Across continents - Tokyo to Toronto. If it's cultural, it disappears. If it's biological, it persists.
  • Control everything - Age, wealth, education. Strip away every excuse.
  • Hide nothing - All data public. All methods transparent. Science as it should be.
  • Replicate relentlessly - Not once. Not twice. Until doubt becomes impossible.

When you eliminate every comfortable excuse, only uncomfortable truth remains.
