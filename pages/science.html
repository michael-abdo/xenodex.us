THE DISCOVERY

The breakthrough came not from new technology, but from understanding why existing approaches consistently failed. Traditional genetic analysis treats DNA as a static code to be decoded, when it should be understood as a dynamic system with contextual meaning.

Our methodology doesn't require more data—it requires better interpretation of the data we already have. Instead of searching for patterns in isolation, we analyze genetic information within its biological, environmental, and temporal context.

This shift in approach transforms genetic analysis from statistical correlation hunting into systematic scientific investigation.

Context reveals what computation cannot see.
